Getting Your Water Intake Tracker On Your Phone
================================================

What You've Built
-----------------
A water intake tracker web app. It's a single HTML page with a glass
that fills up as you log water in ml.

Where It Lives
--------------
Your code is on GitHub at lochlan800/Waterintake on the branch
claude/water-intake-tracker-LMCBC.


Step-by-Step: Getting It On Your Phone
--------------------------------------

Step 1: Merge the branch (on GitHub)

  1. Go to github.com/lochlan800/Waterintake
  2. You should see a banner saying the branch has recent pushes
     — click "Compare & pull request"
  3. Click "Create pull request", then "Merge pull request"
  4. This puts your code on the main branch, which GitHub Pages needs


Step 2: Turn on GitHub Pages

  1. On your repo page, click Settings (top menu)
  2. In the left sidebar, click Pages
  3. Under "Source", select Deploy from a branch
  4. Pick main branch, / (root) folder
  5. Click Save
  6. Wait 1-2 minutes — GitHub will give you a URL like:
     https://lochlan800.github.io/Waterintake/


Step 3: Open on your phone

  1. Open that URL in your phone's browser
  2. iPhone: tap the Share button (square with arrow)
     > "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Android: tap the 3-dot menu > "Install app"
     or "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Now it's on your home screen like a real app!
